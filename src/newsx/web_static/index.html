About TrueNews

An open, verifiable daily news platform that prioritizes trust, provenance, and calm reporting over speed or engagement.

1

Extracts Atomic Facts

Every incoming article is decomposed into discrete factual claims (who, what, when, where, numbers) while discarding rhetoric and speculative framing.

2

Removes Loaded Language

Sensationalized adjectives, partisan verbs, and emotional framing are systematically neutralized to produce calm, factual summaries.

3

Counts Independent Sources

Syndicated wire copies are traced back to their original source so republished wire articles are never counted as separate corroboration.

4

Assigns Clear Trust Scores

Each story and individual fact is labeled with a 5-tier confidence score. When newsrooms report conflicting numbers, an open dispute is clearly flagged.

Open Source & Zero Recurring Cost

TrueNews is completely open-source and operates locally without expensive proprietary AI APIs.
