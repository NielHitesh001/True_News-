    EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

    How TrueNews Works

    TrueNews (Antigravity NewsX) is an open-source, verifiable editorial engine built on strict zero-paid-API principles.

    01

    Atomic Fact Extraction

    Articles are broken down into discrete factual claims (who, what, when, where, how much). Opinion, hyperbole, and rhetorical framing are isolated from the factual base.

    02

    Neutralization & Bias Removal

    Sensationalized verbs, loaded intensifiers, and scare quotes are systematically replaced with neutral equivalents while preserving 100% of underlying facts.

    03

    Syndication Collapse

    Republished wire stories are traced back to their parent wire origin so identical copy isn't mistakenly counted as separate corroboration.

    04

    5-Tier Confidence Scoring

    Claims corroborated by 2+ independent primary origins receive Tier 1 status. Contradictions between sources trigger an active dispute and downgrade.

    05

    Zero Recurring Cost

    Operates completely on local SQLite, deterministic extractors, or local models. No proprietary paid AI APIs, ensuring permanent independence and auditability.

    06

    Complete Provenance Trail

    Every sentence in a TrueNews brief can be drilled down to its exact source article, passage ID, and original unmodified paragraph.
